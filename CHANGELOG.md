# Baby Chicken Empire: what's new

The same list is in the game: tap the version number on the front screen, or open **What’s new**.

## 58. Tidier screen-edge tags (14 Nov 2026)
- Tags pointing the same way (a snake, your pin, treasure, Pip’s cart) no longer pile on top of each other at the edge of the screen: they line up so you can read them all.

## 57. Startled babies (14 Nov 2026)
- When one of your babies spots a snake or fox and starts to run, a ❗ pops up over it, so you can see where to help.

## 56. Catching up on updates (14 Nov 2026)
- If you missed a few updates, the message when you start says how many, and What’s new highlights every one you haven’t seen yet.

## 55. Glowing snake eyes (14 Nov 2026)
- Snakes’ big yellow eyes now glow in the dark, so you can spot them coming at night.

## 54. Peaceful fixes (14 Nov 2026)
- Peaceful now really keeps foxes and crows away too, not just snakes.
- Peaceful nights no longer count for the night goals or the Safe nights total.
- Playing together, the host’s Peaceful setting is the one that counts, and guests now see the right night message.
- Only snakes popped near you count towards your “popped in one night” goal and tally.
- Swapping hotbars shows “⚔️ Fight hotbar” or “🔨 Build hotbar” above it for a moment.

## 53. Prizes in the confetti (14 Nov 2026)
- Pop a snake and there’s often something left in its confetti for anyone close by: pebbles, a bone, apples, a confetti popper or a lamp.
- Finishing a goal sets off a little firework in front of you.

## 52. Middle click to pick a block (14 Nov 2026)
- Keyboard and mouse: middle click a block to put that kind in your hand, if you have some. It jumps to the slot it’s already in, or takes the slot you’re on.

## 51. Which way the snake is coming (14 Nov 2026)
- When a snake turns up, a message says which kind it is and which side of the nursery it’s coming from, like “A boom snake is coming from the south-east!”. North is up, like on the map.

## 50. Map pins (14 Nov 2026)
- Tap anywhere on the map to drop a 📍 pin. A blue tag then points the way and counts down the metres, and when you get there it says so. Tap the pin on the map to pick it up again.

## 49. Peaceful nights (14 Nov 2026)
- New in Settings: Peaceful. No snakes, foxes or crows come, so little ones can build and raise babies without any scares. The clock shows a 🕊️ at night. Peaceful nights don’t count towards a crown.

## 48. Hotbar names and sleepy babies (14 Nov 2026)
- When you pick something on the hotbar, its name and how many you have pop up above it for a moment.
- Babies sleeping near you at night puff a little “z z z”.

## 47. Lamp light slows snakes (14 Nov 2026)
- Snakes squint in the light: near a lamp post or campfire they slow down a bit (a scarecrow still slows them most).
- A soft chime plays with the “sun is setting” warning.
- Out of pebbles? You grab your sword automatically, if it’s on the hotbar.
- Holding a slingshot or fishing rod you haven’t made yet now says how to get one, instead of “you’re out of”.

## 46. Your story so far, and two new goals (14 Nov 2026)
- New in the pause menu: Your story so far. See what day it is, how many animals are in your nursery, your safe nights, the snakes you’ve bonked, the blocks you’ve dug, the goals you’ve done and more.
- Two new goals: pop 3 snakes into confetti in one night, and bonk 25 foxes, crows and snakes in all.
- Goal rewards now read properly, like “2 fireworks and 3 apples”.
- The garden goal says where to find clay: dig sand, or the bottom of deep water.

## 45. Snake alerts and fight trades (14 Nov 2026)
- If a snake sneaks up on your nursery while you’re far away, a message tells you to hurry back.
- At night the clock shows how many snakes are still about, and sunrise tells you how many you popped.
- Pip’s cart can now offer pebbles (6 cobble for 12), confetti poppers (4 apples for 2) and fireworks (6 wheat and 2 eggs for 2).

## 44. A day clock, snake finder and gentler first nights (14 Nov 2026)
- A clock at top left shows ☀️ Day, 🌅 Dusk or 🌙 Night and which day it is. About 20 seconds before dusk a message warns you, and reminds you to swap to the fight hotbar.
- A red 🐍 Snake tag shows how far away the nearest snake (or 🦊 fox) is, and points to it from the edge of the screen when it’s out of sight. Snakes and foxes now show on the map too.
- The first two nights are gentler: each snake takes one bonk less. Every bonk now shows how many more it needs, like “BONK! 2 more”.
- Digging stone turns up a few pebbles now and then, and digging sand sometimes finds a lump of clay.
- New things go to the right hotbar: swords, slingshots, pebbles and poppers to the fight hotbar, building things to the build one.

## 43. Fight hotbar, a slingshot and tougher snakes (10 Nov 2026)
- Two hotbars: tap Fight at the end of the hotbar (or press Q) to swap to your fighting things, and Build to swap back. Each keeps its own items.
- New slingshot (Wood & tools, at a table: 3 sticks, 1 wool): hold it and tap to fling a pebble a long way. Make pebbles from cobble (2 make 6). You learn it from the grow-up goal.
- New confetti popper (Wood & tools, at a table: 1 stick, 2 sand, 1 yellow flower make 2): throw it and it bursts where it lands, bonking every fox, crow or snake close by. You learn it from the firework goal.
- Snakes are tougher: most take 3 hits, diggers 5 and boom snakes 4. They’re a bit faster, come more often and up to 5 a night. Boom snakes fizz for less time and blast a bigger hole.
- Two new goals: hit a raider with a slingshot pebble, and catch one in a popper’s burst.

## 42. Scrolling on phones turned sideways (10 Nov 2026)
- The map, Pip’s cart, the sign and job cards now scroll with your finger when they’re taller than the screen. Before, only the bag, menu and wardrobe would.
- The map shrinks to fit a short screen, so you can see all of it and the key below.
- On a short screen the bag’s tabs stay on one row (swipe it sideways if they don’t all fit) and the panels are a little tighter, leaving more room for your things.
- The “Generating world…” label no longer sits on top of the bag or map.

## 41. More to build, and easier controls (10 Nov 2026)
- Five new things to build (Building tab): stone bricks from cobble, framed windows, lamp posts that glow at night, flower pots, and benches you can sit on. You learn them from the bonk, house, night, treasure chest and sign goals.
- Five new goals: build with 20 stone bricks, put in 4 windows, put up 3 lamp posts, put out 4 flower pots and sit on a bench.
- When you look at something you can use, a label under the middle of the screen says what tapping (or right clicking) does: open, sit, sleep, ring, pick, plant, fill and more.
- Keyboard and mouse: the top buttons show their keys (P photo, M map, E bag, Esc pause), the hotbar shows 1–9, and the main controls show along the bottom when you start. E or Esc closes the bag, and Esc closes the map and Pip’s cart. In the bag, point at something and press 1–9 to put it on that slot.
- In the bag, things you can make right now come first, cards say when no table is needed, and recipes you haven’t learned say which goal teaches them.

## 40. Longer goals, bigger builds (10 Nov 2026)
- Many goals now ask for more: dig 10 blocks, tame 3 babies, chop 4 logs, pick 6 flowers, hatch 5 babies, fill a feeder 3 times, pick wheat 3 times, bonk 3 raiders, keep the nursery safe 2 nights, grow up 2 babies, pick up 10 gifts, catch 5 things fishing, open 2 chests, cook 3 things, make 2 swaps with Pip, bounce 10 times, catch 3 fireflies, find 2 hiding babies and swim 60 blocks.
- Some things cost more to make: a house kit is 32 wood, 4 glass and 2 lamps; a bed is 4 wood and 4 wool; a party cake is 4 wheat, 2 milk and 3 eggs; a fishing rod is 4 sticks and 2 wool; ladders take 8 sticks; fences come 3 at a time; a bouncy pad makes 1.
- The scarecrow, dinner bell and music box need a bit more too, a snowman takes 5 snow and a sandcastle 6 sand.
- Pip asks 9 logs and 3 fish for a house kit, and 8 eggs for a party cake.

## 39. Fairer goals (10 Nov 2026)
- The bouncy pad, snowman and swim ring goals no longer wait on a lucky recipe page. The goal that teaches each one now also teaches the coloured wool it needs (pink, orange or red) and gives you enough to make it straight away.
- Pip now asks 6 logs and 2 fish for a house kit (it was 12 logs), closer to what making one yourself costs.
- A baby playing hide and seek now keeps still and quiet once it has hidden, instead of wandering about and hopping.
- The snowman and sandcastle goals give new rewards to match.

## 38. Hide and seek, a swim ring and sandcastles (10 Nov 2026)
- Hide and seek! Now and then by day a baby from your nursery runs off to hide by a tree, a wall or under some leaves. Listen for its giggles, louder as you get close, and walk right up to it to find it and get a little reward.
- New swim ring (Wood & tools tab, at a crafting table: 2 red wool, 2 wool): hold it in the water and you float on top and swim over twice as fast. Jump to hop out onto the bank. You learn it from the bottle goal.
- New sandcastle (Building tab: 4 sand, 1 stick, 1 red flower) with four towers and a red flag. You learn it from the snowman goal.
- Three new goals: find a baby playing hide and seek, swim 30 blocks with the ring and build a sandcastle.

## 37. A music box, messages in bottles and snowmen (10 Nov 2026)
- New music box (Building tab, at a crafting table: 3 wood, 1 lamp, 1 stick): tap it and it plays a little tune, a different one every time. Your animals close by hop and wag along to the beat. You learn it from the firework goal.
- Messages in bottles: now and then your fishing line brings one up. Most are treasure maps to a chest further away than you could see, and the gold Treasure tag points the way until you open it.
- New snowman (Building tab: 3 snow, 2 sticks, 1 orange wool), with a carrot nose, a red scarf and stick arms. Snow is up on the mountains. You learn it from the firefly goal.
- Important messages, like a bottle’s note or a wish, now stay on screen long enough to read.
- Three new goals: play the music box, fish up a bottle and build a snowman.

## 36. Bouncy pads, firefly jars and shooting stars (9 Nov 2026)
- New bouncy pad (Building tab, at a crafting table: 2 pink wool, 2 wood and 1 hay makes 2): land on one and you spring back up. Hold jump as you land to bounce higher and higher. You learn it from the scarecrow goal.
- Firefly jars: at night, hold glass and tap a firefly to catch it in a jar. Put the jar down and it glows like a little lamp.
- Shooting stars streak across clear night skies now and then. Look right at one before it fades to make a wish, and a little present turns up in your bag.
- Three new goals: bounce on a bouncy pad, catch a firefly and wish on a shooting star.

## 35. Scarecrows, a dinner bell and fireworks (9 Nov 2026)
- New scarecrow (Farm tab, at a crafting table): stand one by your nursery and block snakes that come near it creep along at under half speed. You learn it from the cooking goal.
- New dinner bell (Farm tab): tap it and every animal in your nursery comes running to stand round it. Handy before night falls! You learn it by swapping with Pip.
- Fireworks! Make three at a crafting table from a stick, sand and a red and yellow flower, then tap to launch one. It whooshes up and bursts into colour, and your animals hop about. Best at night. You learn them by throwing a party.
- A moon badge next to your crown counts your perfect nights in a row, and how many the next crown needs.
- Four new goals: make a scarecrow, ring the dinner bell, launch a firework and keep every baby safe 3 nights in a row.
- Scarecrows and bells show on the map.

## 34. Crowns you have to earn (9 Nov 2026)
- The gold crown is harder now: keep every baby safe for 3 nights in a row, with at least 8 animals in your nursery (raising 100 chicks still works too).
- Keep the run going for two new crowns: the ruby crown (5 nights in a row, 20 animals) and the diamond crown (8 nights, 40 animals). Your crown changes colour as it grows.
- One spooked baby starts the run again, and a night slept through in bed doesn’t count. Every sunrise tells you how close the next crown is.
- Crowns you already earned stay yours.

## 33. A map, and Pip the travelling goose (9 Nov 2026)
- New map button (top right, or press M): see the land around you from above, with your nursery, signs, beds, campfires, treasure chests and friends marked on it. Zoom out to see further.
- Every other day, Pip the travelling goose pulls her market cart up near your nursery. Follow the 🛒 tag and tap her to swap things.
- Pip has five swaps each visit, always including a recipe page: eggs for a party cake, fish for lamps, logs for a house kit and more. She leaves in the early afternoon.
- A new goal: swap something with Pip.

## 32. Cooking, the theme song, and keeping your world safe (8 Nov 2026)
- Cooking! Make a campfire from 2 logs and 3 cobble, then cook in the bag’s new Cooking tab: bread, grilled fish, apple pie and a party cake.
- Eat bread to run faster, grilled fish to jump twice as high, or apple pie for both. A little timer shows how long it lasts.
- A party cake throws a party: your animals close by hop about with hearts, and the babies grow a good bit.
- The Baby Chicken Empire song now plays on the menu, and now and then while you play. Switch it off in Settings (Theme song).
- Keeping your world safe: the game asks your browser not to clear it, and the menu reminds you to download a backup now and then.

## 31. Beds, signs and mountain snow (8 Nov 2026)
- Make a bed from 3 wood and 3 wool. Tap it when it’s dark to sleep till morning. Playing together, anyone can go to bed and everyone wakes up at sunrise.
- Put down a sign and write on it. Your words float over it when you’re close by, so you can name your farm, your pens and your house. Tap a sign to change what it says.
- Up high in the snowy mountains, showers fall as gentle snowflakes instead of rain.
- Learn the sign by opening a treasure chest and the bed by putting a hat on an animal (or find their recipe pages). Two new goals go with them.

## 30. Save and load, and coloured wool (8 Nov 2026)
- New on the menu: Saved games. Save the world you’re playing in one of 5 slots, give it a name, and load it again any time.
- Download a backup to keep your world safe in a file, or to move it to another phone or computer. Load a backup brings it back (with the clothes patterns you’ve learned too).
- Your world still saves itself as you play, and loading always asks first, so nothing gets lost by accident.
- Dye wool to build in colour: red, orange, yellow, pink and green wool, made from 2 wool and a flower (or clover). Learn each one from a recipe page.

## 29. Hats for your animals (8 Nov 2026)
- Your animals can wear hats now! Tap one of your babies twice, or a grown-up once, and pick a hat on its card.
- Any hat you’ve sewn in the Wardrobe can go on any animal: a party hat on a hen, a flower crown on a cow, bunny ears on a wolf…
- Everyone playing together sees the hats, and they’re kept in your save.
- New goal: put a hat on one of your animals.

## 28. Treasure chests (8 Nov 2026)
- Treasure chests are hidden about the world, out on the grass, sand and snow. When one is close by, a gold Treasure tag shows you the way.
- Tap a chest to open it: inside are two goodies (apples, wool, eggs, fish, a lamp…) and, most of the time, a recipe page.
- New goal: find a treasure chest and open it.

## 27. Gone fishing (8 Nov 2026)
- New: the fishing rod. Learn it from a recipe page (or the sewing goal), then make it at a crafting table from 3 sticks and 1 wool.
- Hold the rod and tap to cast into a pond, river or the sea. When the float bobs under, tap quickly to reel it in!
- You mostly catch fish, sometimes a treat or some sticks, and now and then a soggy recipe page. Fish bite sooner in the rain and at night.
- Any grown-up will take a fish to learn a job, if you’ve none of its favourite treat.
- New goal: catch something with a fishing rod.

## 26. Learn recipes and patterns (8 Nov 2026)
- You don’t know how to make everything any more. A new world starts with just wood, sticks, the crafting table, fences, seeds and hay.
- Learn more from recipe pages. They turn up now and then when you dig leaves, grass, stone, sand, clay and snow, and many goals teach you something new when you finish them.
- Recipes you haven’t learned show in your bag as grey cards marked “Not learned yet”.
- Clothes need a pattern too. Only the T-shirt, shorts, no hat and no bag are free; for the rest, find the pattern on a recipe page, then sew it in the Wardrobe from wool and flowers.
- Your saved world keeps every recipe you’ve already used, and you keep any clothes you’ve sewn or are wearing.

## 25. Name your animals (8 Nov 2026)
- Give any baby or grown-up your own name for it. Pat a baby twice to open its card, or tap a grown-up, then type in the Name box.
- Its new name shows in all the messages, is kept in your save, and everyone playing together sees it.
- Birds sing by day and crickets chirp at night when you’re outside.

## 24. Pets, butterflies and fireflies (8 Nov 2026)
- New job: Follow me. A grown-up you teach it to trots after you everywhere, day and night, and sits by you when you stop. If you run far ahead it catches up.
- Butterflies flutter round the wild flowers by day, and sometimes land on one.
- Fireflies drift and blink over the grass at night, with glowing tails.

## 23. Sew your own clothes (8 Nov 2026)
- New clothes to sew in the Wardrobe: a chick jumper, a hero cape, dungarees, a tutu, a chick hat (a little chick sits on your head!), a party hat, bunny ears and a flower crown.
- Tap a piece with a padlock to see what it needs, then tap Sew it! Wool comes from grown-up sheep, and flowers grow wild.
- Once you’ve sewn something it’s yours to wear any time, in any colour.
- New goal: sew something new in the Wardrobe.
- The Wardrobe camera steps back a little so tall hats and ears fit.

## 22.1. Wolf and job fixes (8 Nov 2026)
- Fixed: a grown wolf chasing a fox could follow it a very long way from home. It now gives up after about 20 blocks and trots back.
- New goal: teach a grown-up a job.
- When your first baby grows up, you’re told you can tap it to teach it a job.

## 22. Working animals (8 Nov 2026)
- Teach your grown-ups a job! Tap a hen, cow, pig, sheep or wolf and pick Fetch wood, Fetch stone or Find seeds. It costs one of its favourite treats.
- By day a worker finds a tree, bare rock or grass near you, works at it, then carries a log, some cobble or seeds back on its back and drops it at your feet.
- Workers find their way round hills, fences and water. If one can’t find anything to do, it tells you.
- Pick Just play to give it a day off. Jobs save, and friends you play with can teach jobs too.

## 21.1. No more flying (8 Nov 2026)
- You can’t fly any more: the Fly button and the F key are gone. Walk, jump, swim and climb ladders instead.
- If you were up in the air, you drop back down to the ground.

## 21. New kinds of snake (8 Nov 2026)
- A new kind of snake joins each night.
- Poison snake: leaves a trail of green poison goo that lasts 5 seconds. Babies run from it, and it makes you slow.
- Flying snake: flaps over fences and walls, then swoops down on a baby.
- Digger snake: wears a hard hat and chews straight through walls. It takes three bonks.
- Boom snake: fizzes next to a baby, then goes BOOM and blasts the blocks around it. Bonk it while it fizzes to put the fuse out!

## 20. Wardrobe: your name and clothes (8 Nov 2026)
- Open the Wardrobe from the front screen or the pause menu to give yourself a name and change your clothes.
- T-shirts, stripes, hoodies and dresses; shorts, trousers and skirts; caps, beanies, bows and flowers, in 12 colours. Pick your shoe colour and whether you carry your bag.
- You can see yourself while you dress up: drag to turn round, or tap Photo for a picture of your outfit. Tap Surprise me! for a random outfit.
- Friends you play with see your name over your head and the clothes you picked.
- Lachie or Charli is now picked in the Wardrobe too.
- The pause menu says Paused, and shows the version and What’s new.
- Fixed: an open door looked squashed with its handle sticking out. It now swings flat against the side of the doorway.

## 19.1. Bag scrolling fix (8 Nov 2026)
- Fixed: on some Android phones (like Xiaomi) the bag and crafting lists ran off the bottom of the screen and wouldn’t scroll. They now fit the screen and scroll.

## 19. Names, rain and rainbows (8 Nov 2026)
- Every baby has its own name, like Nugget or Pudding. You’ll see it when you pet one or when it grows up.
- Rain showers now and then: the sky turns grey and it stays dry under a roof. Wheat grows faster in the rain.
- When a shower ends on a sunny day, look away from the sun for a rainbow.
- Wander far from your nursery and an arrow at the edge of the screen points the way home.
- The front screen is a little wider.

## 18. Planters, petting and photos (8 Nov 2026)
- The version number is on the front screen, with this list of what’s new.
- Planter boxes: fill one with seeds and wheat grows in it. Tap it when it’s golden to pick it.
- Pet your babies: tap one in your nursery (not holding a treat or the sword). Pats help them grow a little.
- Take a photo of your world with the camera button.
- Each sunrise tells you which day it is.

## 17. Doors and houses (8 Nov 2026)
- Two-tall wooden doors you tap to open and shut.
- A house kit builds a cosy cottage. Babies shut inside are safe from the night snake.

## 16. Babies grow up (8 Nov 2026)
- Tame babies grow up into hens, cows, pigs, sheep and wolves.
- Grown-ups leave gifts: eggs that hatch chicks, milk that helps babies grow, wool and apples.
- Grown wolves guard the nursery from foxes and snakes.

## 15. Goals and flowers (8 Nov 2026)
- A goal list with rewards to lead you through the game.
- Wild flowers to pick and plant, and little pop-ups when you collect things.

## 14. Better crafting (8 Nov 2026)
- Picture cards in tabs, make lots at once, a crafting table, ladders and feeders.

## 13. Fences, crafting and day raiders (8 Nov 2026)
- Wooden fences and gates, collecting things by digging, and crafting.
- Sneaky foxes and treat-pinching crows by day.

## 12. Installs as an app (8 Nov 2026)
- Install it to your home screen as its own app, on any phone.

## 11. Play together fixes (8 Nov 2026)
- Guests see all the animals, and arrows point to friends off screen.

## 10. Smoother play together (8 Nov 2026)
- Friends no longer look frozen to the host.

## 9. Play together (8 Nov 2026)
- Host your world with a room code. Play as Lachie or Charli.

## 8. Baby Chicken Empire (8 Nov 2026)
- The game gets its name, the snake gets three times bigger, and the iPad controls are fixed.

## 7. Chicken king (7 Nov 2026)
- Earn the gold crown and sit on your hay bale throne while the babies cheer.

## 6. The night (7 Nov 2026)
- The square moon, the block snake and the toy sword.

## 5. More babies (7 Nov 2026)
- Calves, piglets, lambs and wolf pups join the nursery.

## 4. Faster updates (7 Nov 2026)
- New versions reach phones straight away.

## 3. Baby chicks (7 Nov 2026)
- Seeds, taming, hatching and the nursery counter.
