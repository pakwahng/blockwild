Controls

Baby animals: each baby has a favourite treat: seeds for chicks, wheat for calves, apples for piglets, clover for lambs and a bone for wolf pups. Hold a treat and those babies follow you. Tap to throw it: a baby that eats one joins your nursery, and two fed babies of the same kind make a new baby. Calves like to munch beside hay and piglets love to roll in mud. Animals can’t hop over wood, hay or walls, so build a pen to keep them.

Night: when the square moon comes up, the babies doze off and a block snake slithers towards your nursery. If it reaches a baby, the baby gets spooked and runs off. Snakes can slither over a one-block fence, but not a two-block wall. Pick the toy sword and tap the snake twice to bonk it into confetti.

Chicken king: keep every baby safe through a night (or raise 100 chicks) to earn the gold crown and a hay bale throne. Place the throne, then tap it to sit: your babies gather round to cheer. Walk or jump to stand up.

Play together: open Play together and tap Host my world to get a four-letter room code. Friends type the code and tap Join to come into your world as Lachie or Charli (pick in Settings). Everyone shares the same nursery, treats and night snakes. Your friends’ own worlds stay safe on their devices.

Phone: drag on the left side to walk (push far to sprint), drag on the right side to look. Tap to place the selected block, press and hold to break. Use the arrow buttons to jump or fly.

Keyboard: WASD to move, mouse to look, left click to break, right click to place, Space to jump, Ctrl to sprint, F to fly, Shift to fly down, 1–9 or the wheel for blocks, E for the block list.

Your world saves on this device automatically.
