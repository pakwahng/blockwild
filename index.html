Saved games

Your world saves itself as you play. To keep a copy, save it in a slot, then load it any time. Download a backup to keep it safe or move it to another device.

Saving and loading are off while you play together.

Settings
Controls

Baby animals: each baby has a favourite treat: seeds for chicks, wheat for calves, apples for piglets, clover for lambs and a bone for wolf pups. Hold a treat and those babies follow you. Tap to throw it: a baby that eats one joins your nursery, and two fed babies of the same kind make a new baby. Calves like to munch beside hay and piglets love to roll in mud. Animals can’t hop over wood, hay or walls, so build a pen to keep them.

Growing up: babies in your nursery slowly grow up (about seven minutes) into a hen, cow, pig, sheep or wolf. Grown-ups leave gifts on the ground: walk over them to pick them up. Hens lay eggs (throw one and a chick hatches where it lands), cows give milk (throw it near a baby and it drinks it to grow up faster), sheep leave wool for building and pigs dig up apples. Grown wolves guard the nursery and chase off foxes and snakes.

Jobs: tap a grown-up to teach it a job. It costs one of its favourite treats. Workers fetch wood (logs), fetch stone (cobble) or find seeds near you by day and carry what they get back to you on their back. Or teach it to follow you everywhere, like a pet. Tap it again and pick Just play to stop.

Saved games: your world saves itself, but on the menu you can also save it in one of 5 slots and load it again later, or download a backup file to keep it safe or move it to another device. The menu reminds you now and then to download a backup.

Theme song: the Baby Chicken Empire song plays on the menu, and now and then while you play. Switch it off in Settings.

Treasure: chests are hidden about the world. When one is near, a gold Treasure tag points the way. Tap it to open it for goodies and, most times, a recipe page.

Map: tap the map button (or press M) for a bird’s-eye map: your nursery, signs, beds, campfires, treasure and friends.

Pip the travelling goose: every other day she brings her cart near your nursery. Follow the 🛒 tag and tap her to swap spare things for useful ones, and always a recipe page.

Cooking: make a campfire (2 logs, 3 cobble) and cook on it in the bag’s Cooking tab. Hold bread, grilled fish or apple pie and tap to eat it: you run faster or jump higher for a while. A party cake throws a party for your animals, and the babies grow a little.

Beds and signs: make a bed (4 wood, 4 wool) and tap it when it’s dark to sleep till morning. Put a sign down to write on it: the words float above it when you’re close. Tap a sign to change what it says.

Fishing: once you’ve learned the fishing rod, make it at a crafting table. Hold it and tap to cast into water. When the float bobs under, tap quickly to reel in a fish (or something else!). Any grown-up will take a fish to learn a job.

Names and hats: pat a baby twice, or tap a grown-up, and type your own name for it in the Name box. Pick a Hat for it too: any hat you’ve sewn in the Wardrobe.

Goals: the star line under your nursery counters shows your next goal. Tap it to see them all. Each goal you finish gives you a reward. Wild flowers grow on the grass: dig them to pick them and place them to plant them.

Collect and make: you start with seeds and the toy sword. Dig blocks to collect them (grass can turn up seeds, wheat and clover, leaves can drop apples and sticks, dirt sometimes hides a bone). Open your bag (top right) to pick what to hold, and use its tabs to make new things: tap Make, hold it to keep making, or tap All. Wood comes from logs, sticks from wood. Fences, gates, ladders, feeders and the sword need a crafting table close by (make one from 4 wood). At first you only know a few recipes: learn more from recipe pages, which turn up when you dig leaves, grass, stone and sand, and from finishing goals. Tap a gate to open or shut it. Walk into a ladder to climb it. Tap a feeder while holding a treat to fill it, and it puts treats out for the babies.

Planters and pets: make a planter box (3 wood and 2 dirt at the table) and tap it while holding seeds to plant wheat. In a couple of minutes it turns golden: tap it to pick wheat and get some seeds back. Tap a baby in your nursery to pet it (not while holding a treat or the sword). Babies love a pat, and it helps them grow up a little.

Photos: tap the camera button at the top to take a picture of your world. On a phone you can save it or send it to someone.

Doors and houses: make a door at the crafting table (3 wood and a stick). Doors are two blocks tall: tap one to open or shut it. Make a house kit (32 wood, 4 glass and 2 lamps) and put it down on the ground: a cosy cottage builds itself with its door facing you. Shut your babies in a house at night and the snake can’t get them.

Names, rain and rainbows: every baby has its own name. Now and then it rains: wheat grows faster, and it’s dry under a roof. High up in the mountains it snows instead. When a shower ends on a sunny day, look away from the sun for a rainbow. If you wander far from your babies, an arrow at the edge of the screen points the way back to them.

Wardrobe: open it from the front screen or the pause menu. Give yourself a name, pick Lachie or Charli, and choose your top, bottoms, hat, shoes and bag in lots of colours. Drag to turn round and see yourself, or tap Photo. Friends you play with see your name and clothes. Pieces with a padlock you sew yourself. First you need the piece’s pattern, from a recipe page. Then tap it to see what it needs (wool from grown-up sheep, and flowers) and tap Sew it!

Day raiders: by day a sneaky fox creeps up on your nursery to spook a baby, and crows swoop down to pinch treats lying on the ground. Foxes can’t climb fences, so pen your babies in. Bonk a fox twice with the sword for 2 bones, or a crow once for some seeds.

Night: when the square moon comes up, the babies doze off and a block snake slithers towards your nursery. If it reaches a baby, the baby gets spooked and runs off. Snakes can slither over a one-block fence, but not a two-block wall. Pick the toy sword and bonk the snake three times to pop it into confetti, or hit it from further away with a slingshot. Diggers take five hits and boom snakes four. For no snakes at all, turn on Peaceful in Settings. The first two nights are gentler: each snake takes one hit less, and every bonk shows how many more it needs. The clock pill at top left shows day, dusk or night, a message warns you just before dusk, and a red 🐍 tag points to the nearest snake. At night the clock counts the snakes still about, and if one sneaks up on the nursery while you are far away, you get a warning.

Kinds of snake: a new kind of snake joins each night. The poison snake leaves a trail of green goo that lasts 5 seconds: babies run from it and it slows you down. The flying snake flaps over fences and walls and swoops down on a baby. The digger snake in its hard hat chews straight through walls. The boom snake fizzes next to a baby and goes BOOM, blasting the blocks around it, so bonk it while it fizzes to put the fuse out!

Scarecrows, bells and fireworks: stand a scarecrow by your nursery and snakes near it slow right down. Lamp posts and campfires slow them a little too. Tap a dinner bell and your animals all come running to it. Tap with a firework to launch it: it bursts into colour, best at night. The 🌙 badge counts your perfect nights in a row.

Bouncy pads, fireflies and wishes: land on a bouncy pad to spring up, and hold jump as you land to go higher. At night, hold glass and tap a firefly to catch it in a glowing jar. If you see a shooting star, look right at it to make a wish!

Music, bottles and snowmen: tap a music box and your animals dance along. Fishing sometimes brings up a message in a bottle: follow the Treasure tag to the chest it describes. Build a snowman from snow, sticks and orange wool.

Hide and seek, swimming and sandcastles: when a baby runs off to hide, follow its giggles and walk up to it to find it. Hold a swim ring in the water to float and swim fast. Build sandcastles from sand, a stick and a red flower.

More to build: stone bricks from cobble, framed windows, lamp posts that glow at night, flower pots, and benches you can sit on (tap one). You learn them from goals: bonking a raider, building a house, keeping the nursery safe at night, finding a chest and writing on a sign. When you look at something you can use, a label under the middle of the screen says what tapping it does.

Build and fight hotbars: you have two hotbars. Tap the Fight button at the end of the hotbar (or press Q) to swap to your fighting things: sword, slingshot, pebbles, confetti poppers and snacks. Tap Build to swap back. Hold the slingshot and tap to fling a pebble a long way (make pebbles from cobble, or dig stone and find a few). Throw a confetti popper and it bursts where it lands, bonking every fox, crow or snake close by.

Chicken king: keep every baby safe for 3 nights in a row with at least 8 animals in your nursery (or raise 100 chicks) to earn the gold crown and a hay bale throne. Keep the run going for the ruby crown (5 nights, 20 animals) and the diamond crown (8 nights, 40 animals). One spooked baby starts the run again, and nights you sleep through don't count. Place the throne, then tap it to sit: your babies gather round to cheer. Walk or jump to stand up.

Play together: open Play together and tap Host my world to get a four-letter room code. Friends type the code and tap Join to come into your world as Lachie or Charli (pick in Settings). Everyone shares the same nursery, treats and night snakes. Your friends’ own worlds stay safe on their devices.

Phone: drag on the left side to walk (push far to sprint), drag on the right side to look. Tap to place the selected block or open a gate, press and hold to dig. Use the arrow button to jump.

Keyboard: WASD to move, mouse to look, left click to break, right click to place, Space to jump, Ctrl to sprint, Shift to climb down a ladder, 1–9 or the wheel to pick from the hotbar, E for your bag (E or Esc closes it), Q to swap between the build and fight hotbars, M for the map, P for a photo, Esc to pause. In the bag, point at something and press 1–9 to put it on that hotbar slot. A label under the crosshair tells you what right clicking will do.

Your world saves on this device automatically.
