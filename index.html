Baby animals: each baby has a favourite treat: seeds for chicks, wheat for calves, apples for piglets, clover for lambs and a bone for wolf pups. Hold a treat and those babies follow you. Tap to throw it: a baby that eats one joins your nursery, and two fed babies of the same kind make a new baby. Calves like to munch beside hay and piglets love to roll in mud. Animals can’t hop over wood, hay or walls, so build a pen to keep them.

Growing up: babies in your nursery slowly grow up (about seven minutes) into a hen, cow, pig, sheep or wolf. Grown-ups leave gifts on the ground: walk over them to pick them up. Hens lay eggs (throw one and a chick hatches where it lands), cows give milk (throw it near a baby and it drinks it to grow up faster), sheep leave wool for building and pigs dig up apples. Grown wolves guard the nursery and chase off foxes and snakes.

Goals: the star line under your nursery counters shows your next goal. Tap it to see them all. Each goal you finish gives you a reward. Wild flowers grow on the grass: dig them to pick them and place them to plant them.

Collect and make: you start with seeds and the toy sword. Dig blocks to collect them (grass can turn up seeds, wheat and clover, leaves can drop apples and sticks, dirt sometimes hides a bone). Open your bag (top right) to pick what to hold, and use its tabs to make new things: tap Make, hold it to keep making, or tap All. Wood comes from logs, sticks from wood. Fences, gates, ladders, feeders and the sword need a crafting table close by (make one from 4 wood). Tap a gate to open or shut it. Walk into a ladder to climb it. Tap a feeder while holding a treat to fill it, and it puts treats out for the babies.

Planters and pets: make a planter box (3 wood and 2 dirt at the table) and tap it while holding seeds to plant wheat. In a couple of minutes it turns golden: tap it to pick wheat and get some seeds back. Tap a baby in your nursery to pet it (not while holding a treat or the sword). Babies love a pat, and it helps them grow up a little.

Photos: tap the camera button at the top to take a picture of your world. On a phone you can save it or send it to someone.

Doors and houses: make a door at the crafting table (3 wood and a stick). Doors are two blocks tall: tap one to open or shut it. Make a house kit (20 wood, 2 glass and a lamp) and put it down on the ground: a cosy cottage builds itself with its door facing you. Shut your babies in a house at night and the snake can’t get them.

Day raiders: by day a sneaky fox creeps up on your nursery to spook a baby, and crows swoop down to pinch treats lying on the ground. Foxes can’t climb fences, so pen your babies in. Bonk a fox twice with the sword for 2 bones, or a crow once for some seeds.

Night: when the square moon comes up, the babies doze off and a block snake slithers towards your nursery. If it reaches a baby, the baby gets spooked and runs off. Snakes can slither over a one-block fence, but not a two-block wall. Pick the toy sword and tap the snake twice to bonk it into confetti.

Chicken king: keep every baby safe through a night (or raise 100 chicks) to earn the gold crown and a hay bale throne. Place the throne, then tap it to sit: your babies gather round to cheer. Walk or jump to stand up.

Play together: open Play together and tap Host my world to get a four-letter room code. Friends type the code and tap Join to come into your world as Lachie or Charli (pick in Settings). Everyone shares the same nursery, treats and night snakes. Your friends’ own worlds stay safe on their devices.

Phone: drag on the left side to walk (push far to sprint), drag on the right side to look. Tap to place the selected block or open a gate, press and hold to dig. Use the arrow buttons to jump or fly.

Keyboard: WASD to move, mouse to look, left click to break, right click to place, Space to jump, Ctrl to sprint, F to fly, Shift to fly down, 1–9 or the wheel to pick from the hotbar, E for your bag.

Your world saves on this device automatically.
